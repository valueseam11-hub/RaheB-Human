# Work Identity & Recognition Intelligence v0.1

## Purpose
Capture the difference between a person's formal role expectations and the work they report actually performing as workflows change with AI. Help the owner assemble a clear, evidence-labelled account of added responsibilities, human judgment, AI-assisted work and measurable outcomes.

This is an employee-controlled evidence and preparation capability. It is not an employment-law engine, compensation calculator, performance-rating system, employee-monitoring tool or automated employment-decision system.

## Core records
- **RoleExpectation:** formal or informal expectation, its source and effective date.
- **WorkRealityEntry:** actual work, observation period, frequency, evidence status, source references, whether AI assisted, the human responsibility retained and optional measured outcome.
- **RecognitionCase:** owner, role title, formal expectations, actual work entries, requested discussion topics, preparation time and explicit owner disclosure approval.

Evidence states remain distinct: SELF_REPORTED, DOCUMENTED, CORROBORATED, DISPUTED and NOT_VERIFIED. A documented or corroborated entry must carry evidence references. Measured outcomes require both baseline and observed values plus a unit. The system does not infer causation from a before/after metric.

## Owner-controlled recognition workflow
1. Record the current role description and its source.
2. Record changed or additional work, when it began and how frequently it occurs.
3. Separate AI-assisted preparation from the human's review, correction, judgment, relationship work and decisions.
4. Attach permissible evidence such as approved workflow records, deliverables, manager instructions or outcome reports. Do not copy employer/client confidential data into a personal store without authority.
5. Label every claim by evidence status; preserve disputed and unverified claims.
6. Prepare a neutral discussion summary for the owner to review.
7. Share only selected entries after explicit owner approval.

A potential scope gap is only a prompt for review. It does not prove a contractual variation or entitlement to higher pay, promotion or a new title.

## Recognition topics
The owner may prepare a discussion about responsibility, workload, role scope, skill development, business outcomes, compensation review or title/level review. RaheB does not decide the outcome or predict what an employer will grant.

## Product and workforce safeguards
- No covert monitoring, keystroke capture, worker ranking, replaceability score or layoff prediction.
- No default employer access to personal work records or contribution evidence.
- No automatic résumé claims or publication of achievements.
- No treating AI-generated output as unaided human work or proof of human competence.
- No unsupported attribution of business savings to one employee; record baseline, measurement method, unit, period and relevant workflow context.
- No claim that efficiency guarantees higher pay, job security or bargaining power.
- Respect employer/client confidentiality, intellectual property, privacy, retention and jurisdiction-specific employment rules.

## Relationship to existing RaheB capabilities
This capability connects the Personal Work Representative, Work Intelligence Engine, Human–AI Handover, Professional Growth Engine, Contribution Evidence Engine and WorkloadShift measurement. It uses those records as evidence inputs but does not silently import or disclose them. Existing employee/employer data boundaries and owner consent remain authoritative.

## v0.1 implementation boundary
Implemented as deterministic, in-memory Python data contracts and validation helpers. No database persistence, API, employer integration, document ingestion, compensation benchmarking, legal analysis, automated résumé generation or external disclosure is implemented. Synthetic data only; not production-ready.
