Measure the work AI actually changes.

Before deciding to automate or restructure a role, compare the full human effort before and after AI assistance. Include review, correction, exceptions, coordination and training—not just the time spent generating an output.

Enter a real or synthetic workflow sample to see the full-effort calculation.

Human transition review
  • Have employees been told how AI changes their responsibilities and what remains human-owned?
  • Are AI-use expectations, accountability and compensation implications being discussed transparently?
  • Have review, exception handling, customer relationships and operational follow-through been tested under real conditions?
  • Are junior employees retaining supervised practice and pathways to develop judgment?
  • Have redeployment, reskilling and employee support been considered before role removal?

This calculator is a workflow discussion aid, not an employment decision tool. It does not assess individual workers, recommend layoffs or prove that a role is replaceable. Validate baseline data, service quality, error rates, customer outcomes and longer-term effects before acting. All entries stay in this browser and are not saved or sent.
