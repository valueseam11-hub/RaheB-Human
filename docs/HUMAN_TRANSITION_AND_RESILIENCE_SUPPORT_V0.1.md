# Human Transition & Resilience Support v0.1

## Purpose
Provide a private, person-directed way to record concerns during AI-related work change, choose the kind of support wanted and prepare practical next steps. The layer recognises that a change in tasks or employment can also affect professional identity, belonging, confidence, financial security and a person's sense of direction.

## Do not medicalise the transition
The product must not diagnose "AI grief", assign a person to a Kübler-Ross stage, infer depression or anxiety, or produce a resilience, mental-health or fitness-for-work score. The five-stage grief model may be discussed as one possible descriptive lens in educational material, never as a universal sequence, clinical protocol or employee classification. People respond differently; no stage is required or expected.

## Owner-controlled check-in
A person may optionally record concerns across:
- professional identity and career direction
- financial uncertainty and skill confidence
- workload, burnout, team belonging and trust/transparency
- other concerns in their own words

The person selects desired support: private reflection, exploring options, skill development, career planning, preparing a conversation with a manager/HR, or connecting with human support. No check-in is required to use the career transition tools.

## Human support and safety
RaheB Human is not a clinical service, therapist, crisis service or substitute for qualified care. It should offer clearly labelled pathways to appropriate human support where available, and encourage a qualified professional when distress is significant or persistent. Do not present generic AI responses as therapy or crisis intervention. Urgent safety concerns require a clear handoff to local emergency or qualified human support, not an automated resilience workflow.

## Employer transparency record
A separate organisational record can state:
- confirmed facts
- what remains unknown
- the accountable contact
- when the next update is due

Leaders should not be encouraged to pretend certainty. A useful update distinguishes what is known, what is not known, what is being decided, who is responsible and when employees will hear more. Do not promise roles, compensation or outcomes that have not been approved.

## Privacy and consent
Check-ins are private by default and are not visible to employers, managers or HR. Employer sharing requires explicit, specific owner approval. Do not use check-in data for performance management, worker ranking, redundancy selection, attendance monitoring, insurance, hiring or employability scoring. Minimise retention and provide export/correction/deletion pathways in the eventual persistent implementation.

## Relationship to the RaheB Human architecture
Connects to Work Identity & Recognition, Career Transition, Professional Identity & Expertise, Human Interaction Boundaries and WorkloadShift. It can help a person prepare for a human conversation or choose a career-development action, but it does not silently change their profile or disclose their reflections.

## v0.1 implementation boundary
Deterministic in-memory contracts and a summary helper only. No clinical inference, AI counselling, sentiment analysis, employer dashboard, persistent storage, API, external support directory or emergency-service integration. Synthetic fixtures only; not production-ready.
